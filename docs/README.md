# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains OctoAcme's project management guidance for cross-functional product, service, and integration work. The docs describe a customer-first, iterative approach with clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and continuous learning.

## Project management process summary

OctoAcme manages work through a lightweight lifecycle:

1. **Initiation:** Validate the business need, define measurable outcomes, identify stakeholders, and decide whether the work is ready for planning.
2. **Planning:** Turn approved work into a prioritized backlog, define acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done, estimate work, and map milestones, risks, and dependencies.
3. **Execution and tracking:** Deliver in small increments, coordinate through standups and delivery reviews, track work on a project board, and use CI, reviews, and testing to maintain quality.
4. **Risk and communication:** Maintain and review a risk register, keep stakeholders informed, and escalate blockers through the agreed path.
5. **Release and deployment:** Confirm readiness, deploy with verification and rollback plans, and communicate releases to stakeholders.
6. **Retrospective and improvement:** Review outcomes after sprints, releases, or incidents and track a small set of actionable improvements.

Project Managers coordinate delivery, Product Managers own outcomes and prioritization, developers build and test, QA validates acceptance, and stakeholders provide input and approvals.
